# Troubleshooting guide

Use these checks before contacting support.

## The product will not start

1. Check the power connection.
2. Confirm the outlet is working.
3. Disconnect accessories and try again.
4. Record any indicator lights or error messages.

## A feature is unavailable

- Confirm the feature is enabled in settings.
- Check your access permissions.
- Restart the product if appropriate.

## Contact support

Share the product model, software version, and exact error message.
