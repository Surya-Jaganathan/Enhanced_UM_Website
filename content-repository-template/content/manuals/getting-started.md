# Getting started

Welcome to your product documentation. This guide walks you through the initial setup.

## Before you begin

- Check the items included in the package.
- Keep your product serial number handy.
- Make sure you have a stable power source.

## Set up your product

1. Unpack the product and inspect it for visible damage.
2. Connect the required cables or accessories.
3. Switch on the device and wait for the status indicator.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete setup.

> **Tip:** Keep this guide available for future reference.

## Next steps

Review the troubleshooting guide if you encounter a problem.
